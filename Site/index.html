About the game

One finger. One runner. One very angry volcano.

Magma Run is a pick-up-and-play dodge game with a hand-painted look. Rocks erupt from the crater on real ballistic arcs and rain from the sky as the mountain builds up steam. You only move left and right, but every close call, every ember streak and every lava river turns a simple dodge into a decision.

Real eruptions

Boulders launch with aimed and random trajectories, then speed up and multiply as the eruption grows.

Embers are the economy

Glowing embers drift down mid-run. Catch them to fund the Forge and unlock new runners. They are only ever earned by playing.

Permanent progress

Agility, hearts, a recharging shield, an ember magnet and fortune: five upgrades that make each run longer than the last.

How it works

Three steps, every run.

1

Steer

Drag anywhere and the runner follows your finger. Prefer buttons? Switch to left / right controls in Settings.

2

Dodge & collect

Weave between falling rocks, grab embers and read the banner: the volcano always warns you before it changes.

3

Forge

Spend embers on upgrades and new runners, then go back up the mountain stronger.

Signature mechanics

Six rules of the mountain.

These are not random power-ups. They are systems that feed each other, and learning how they interlock is the whole game.

Rage & Shockwave

Let a boulder graze past instead of running away. Five near misses fill the rage bar and release a shockwave that shatters every rock around you.

Lava Rivers

Cracks start glowing on a strip of ground. Moments later it floods with lava. Standing in it costs a heart, so the safe side keeps changing.

Obsidian Crust New

When a river drains it cools into glossy black crust for a few seconds. Stand on it and your score doubles, and every near miss builds twice the rage.

Overdrive

Six embers in a row without taking damage turn you molten. For five seconds boulders shatter on contact and every ember counts double.

Heart Shards New

Hot, glowing boulders smashed by a shockwave or overdrive drop a red shard. Three shards restore a heart, or pay out embers when you are at full health.

Volcano Moods

A banner warns you, then the whole sky changes. Fury hurls aimed rocks, Ash Rain drops them from above, Ember Shower is your harvest window.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an internet connection to play?

No. Magma Run is fully offline. Upgrades, runners and records live on your device.

Can I buy embers?

No. There are no in-app purchases. Every ember is earned by playing.

What does the obsidian crust do?

After a lava river drains, its strip cools into black crust marked ×2 for a few seconds. While you stand on it your score and rage build twice as fast.

How do I get heart shards?

Smash a hot, glowing boulder with a rage shockwave or while in overdrive. It drops a red shard; collect three to restore a heart.

I switched phones. Where is my progress?

Progress is stored locally on the device. Restore the device from an iCloud or computer backup to bring it along.

How do I reset my progress?

Open Settings inside the game and tap Reset. You will be asked to confirm; it cannot be undone.

Something crashed or looks wrong.

Write to support@magmarun.site with your device model and iOS version, or use reportaproblem.apple.com.
